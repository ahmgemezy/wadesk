## Design Context

### Users
Arabic-speaking business owners and customer support agents at SMBs in Egypt and the Gulf (Saudi, UAE, Kuwait). They manage WhatsApp conversations as a team. They're hands-on, mobile-aware, and not tech-savvy — they need clarity over cleverness. Context: working in a shared inbox, handling dozens of customer conversations per day.

### Brand Personality
Professional · Warm · Efficient

WhatsApp-native feel — familiar green, clean interfaces, no unnecessary friction. The product should feel like a premium Arab SaaS, not a global enterprise tool. Confidence-building for non-technical business owners.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light mode primary (dark mode supported). Clean white surfaces with subtle warm-tinted neutrals. Green accent color (`oklch(0.52 0.16 155)`) as the brand green — not neon, a rich WhatsApp-adjacent green.
- **Tone**: Refined utilitarian — clean and structured like Notion or Linear, but warmer and more approachable. Not cold enterprise. Not playful SaaS.
- **Typography**: Arabic (Cairo/Tajawal) + a refined Latin sans. Clear hierarchy. No display fonts. Functionality-first.
- **Anti-references**: Avoid Duolingo/childish SaaS, avoid dark-mode-with-neon, avoid generic US-startup blue-purple. Never looks like a generic Tailwind template.
- **Memorable element**: RTL-native layouts that feel designed for Arabic, not ported from English. Green as a purposeful brand color everywhere it adds meaning.

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity over density** — Show what matters at a glance; hide complexity behind deliberate progressive disclosure
2. **RTL-native** — Every layout, every margin, every icon direction must feel designed for Arabic first
3. **Green with purpose** — Use the brand green only on primary actions and data highlights, not decoratively
4. **No card inside card** — Flatten visual hierarchy; avoid nested containers and excessive borders
5. **Text earns its place** — No redundant labels, no instructional text that restates the heading
